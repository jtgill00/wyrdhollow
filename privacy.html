Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026

WyrdHollow: Part 1 is made by JT, a solo developer. This page covers the game (in your browser, on itch.io and in the iPhone and iPad app) and this website.

The game

  • The game does not ask for an account, your name, your email or your location.
  • The iPhone and iPad app collects no data and makes no network connections. It has no ads, no analytics and no purchases.
  • Your saved game stays on your own device or in your own browser. Deleting the app or clearing your browser's site data deletes it.

This website

  • When you first visit, the site asks whether you accept cookies. If you decline, no analytics or advertising cookies are set.
  • If you accept, the site uses Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to count visits and how many people press Play, so the developer can tell whether adverts are working. Those services receive standard information such as your browser type, approximate region and the pages you view.
  • You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of the home page.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps ordinary server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
