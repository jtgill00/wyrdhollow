The Game

A wizard's life,
a valley's fate.

A letter sealed in violet wax brings you to WyrdHollow. Your great-aunt has vanished, her cottage is a ruin, and the silver scar on your palm has started to glow.

Light & Shadow

Your spells
decide who you are.

There's no good-or-evil dialogue choice. Radiant magic protects, heals and calms. Hollow magic drains, binds and curses, and it's stronger and quicker to learn. The villagers, your creatures, even the glow of your wand notice which you use.

The World

A valley that opens in rings.

Fifteen places, each unlocked by what you learn, brew, befriend or earn. Tap a light on the map.

The People

Friends, rivals, and something worse.

Befriend them, romance three of them, fight beside them, or against them. Tap anyone to meet them.

Six Guilds

Rise from Initiate to Adept.

Each guild guards one of the six Seals. Earn it with a hand-made trial, or take the guild's hidden Hollow road.

Familiars

Calm them, or bind them.

Kneel and keep still to calm a creature with Radiant patience, or bind it instantly with Hollow magic. Either way it joins you, helps at home and fights at your side.
